⚠️ Data & scoring notes Research and map limitations
Saved listings are research snapshots, not live verified listing data. Card previews use public Texas NAIP 2022 aerial imagery where available (USGS fallback) and saved approximate location pins; imagery dates and resolution vary. The all-property overview uses one USGS topographic road-map image (not hundreds of tiles) and locally plotted saved property pins. Parcel outlines only appear when verified GIS polygons exist; driving times appear when supplied by a routing source. Automatic wishlist scores use only existing documented evidence, not live listing scraping. Two independent scoring lists are retained. Progress bars combine unique topics only; a topic in both lists contributes once. Unknown topics stay in the fixed maximum at 0 earned points until assessed, and are not confirmed deficiencies. Personal notes, ranks, and wishlist ratings are saved separately for each account. Both accounts start from identical evidence-based automatic scores. Shared property changes sync between both accounts.
